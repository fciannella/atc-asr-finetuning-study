# Training data behind the 12 evaluated ATC checkpoints

Reviewed **November 6, 2026**, against the exact model-card revisions saved in the [inventory](community-atc-model-inventory.md), the companion project, and our internal experiment records. The nine cached community cards were checked against their recorded SHA-256 hashes. Links below point to those frozen cards, not an assumed current `main` version.

We can identify the main **ATC adaptation datasets** for all 12 checkpoints, with different levels of evidence. Our three models have recorded training stages and source pools. The other nine rely on publisher descriptions; jlvdoorn's sparse card needs its companion project to identify the corpus. None of this establishes a complete, independently audited list of every sample seen during base pretraining, teacher training, and all adaptation stages.

## Dataset names in plain language

- **ATCO2 Silver:** our large pool of operational ATC recordings with supplied machine transcripts, used for broad adaptation.
- **ATCO2 Gold:** human-accepted ATCO2 labels. Our release candidates use a 0.418-hour training split; the separate two-hour package is the evaluation split. Other publishers' ATCO2 subsets/splits are not automatically the same partitions.
- **Jacktol ATC-ASR:** a public ATC corpus. Our pinned training pool contains 5.896 hours. That is our measured pool size, not a verified amount used by every Jacktol-trained community model. The study established source-recording links to ATCO2.
- **ATCOSIM:** ATC simulation speech. These are human speakers in a simulation, distinct from synthesized speech.
- **UWB:** the University of West Bohemia ATC corpus used in the early stages of our historical P2 curriculum.
- **LibriSpeech:** general-English speech used as replay in our fine-tuning to retain general ASR capability.
- **RadioTalk / STEAK:** synthetic ATC training sources. RASR's RadioTalk card identifies Llama 3.2 dialogue and Tada speech synthesis; STEAK's reviewed card does not provide equivalent generation detail.
- **TartanAviation:** real US radio audio; RASR v2 uses a selected 28.8-hour subset with automatically produced labels.
- **YouTube ATC:** human simulator-session audio with automatically produced transcripts, not operational tower recordings or synthesized speech.

Hours, utterances and augmented-file counts are different quantities. Pool size is also different from exposure: P4/N4 sample Silver/English at 80/20, then Gold/English at 85/15, repeating available examples. RASR v1 upweights its real-data anchors. These ratios do not describe percentages of unique audio duration.

## What this tells us about the benchmark

The [overlap explanation beside the comparison table](evaluation-tracker.md#where-training-and-test-audio-overlap) distinguishes confirmed shared recordings, exact training segments and unresolved ancestry, and defines what the supplementary score excludes.

1. **The leading RASR models are not independent of ATCO2 by construction.** Both include European ATCO2 adaptation. RASR v1 also uses substantial synthetic speech; v2 inherits it and adds US audio. Their strong scores do not prove that synthetic data alone explains the improvement.
2. **Shared dataset names do not mean identical training.** qenneth, Jacktol Whisper and our P2 all use Jacktol, but differ in initialization, other sources, labels, sampling and optimization. P2 includes inherited UWB training.
3. **Different declared sources are useful transfer comparisons, not proof of total independence.** US radio, simulator-only and synthetic-only adaptation may still have relevant base, teacher, text or noise ancestry. The source-level audit is incomplete.
4. **Real audio does not imply human labels.** RASR v2 and YouTube FastConformer use pseudo-labels, and our Silver stage uses supplied machine transcripts. Teacher-model lineage belongs in a complete provenance record.
5. **The older Gold-additive P2 variant is excluded.** Its training includes 554 exact audio segments in our current two-hour test. Its historical 15.44% score used a different 1.48-hour test. See the [split audit](jacktol-p2-current-test-2026-11-06.md).

## What remains unknown

The inventory pins model revisions, not every training dataset revision. Closing the gaps requires training manifests or immutable dataset revisions and split definitions; source-recording/segment matching against our test; earlier checkpoint lineage; and teacher, synthetic-text, TTS and noise-source histories where applicable. Base-pretraining documentation must be reviewed separately. A named corpus or repository license is not an independent verification of contents or release rights.

See the [evaluation tracker](evaluation-tracker.md) for measured scores and remaining work, the [community assessment](community-atc-model-assessment.md) for broader candidates, and the [data contract](experiments/00-data-and-evaluation.md) for our source pools.
